# 1. The Harmonic Oscillator model

The harmonic oscillator is probably one of the most familiar models in physics.

At first glance, it seems almost too simple: a mass is attached to a spring, displaced from its equilibrium position, and then released.

However, the importance of the harmonic oscillator goes far beyond springs.

The same mathematical structure appears in mechanical vibrations, electrical circuits, molecular vibrations, crystal phonons, electromagnetic fields, and eventually quantum electrodynamics.

This raises a natural question:

> **Why does the same equation appear again and again in completely different physical systems?**

That is where we begin.
